About Us

Making AI Workflows Reliable

Founded in 2026, NodeRefine is on a mission to help every team build, debug, and optimize AI workflows with confidence.

Our Mission

As AI workflows become the backbone of modern software, the complexity of debugging them grows exponentially. A single misconfigured node can cascade into failures across an entire pipeline — costing time, money, and user trust.

NodeRefine was built to solve this problem. We provide the diagnostic intelligence that developers and operations teams need to find the weakest node, fix it fast, and keep AI systems running at peak performance.

Our Values

Developer First

Built by engineers, for engineers. Every feature starts with a real debugging pain point.

Privacy by Design

Your data is yours. We never train on customer data and support private deployment.

Radical Transparency

Clear pricing, open documentation, and honest communication about what our platform can and cannot do.

Continuous Improvement

AI evolves fast. We ship weekly and iterate based on real user feedback.
